Chapter 1

Getting Started

1.1 Accessing the Platform

MetricBase is a web application. Open your browser and navigate to the URL provided by your administrator. No additional software installation is required.

Sign-in options

  • Email & Password — Enter your registered email address and password on the sign-in page.
  • Google OAuth — Click Continue with Google to authenticate using your Google account.
  • Microsoft Entra ID — Click Continue with Microsoft for Microsoft/Azure AD sign-in.
  • Magic Link — Enter your email and receive a one-time link via email (no password needed).
Tip All sign-in methods that share the same verified email address are linked to the same account. You can sign in with Google one day and with your password the next — your data is the same either way.

1.2 Navigation Overview

The top navigation bar is always visible. It contains:

  • MetricBase logo — click to return to the Workspace Home dashboard.
  • App Switcher (grid icon) — switch between ProBase, Finance, FieldFlow, OGtools, and Chat without losing your place.
  • Search (⌘K / Ctrl+K) — search tasks, transactions, nodes, and more across all modules.
  • Organization Selector — if you belong to multiple organizations, switch between them here. All data is organization-scoped.
  • Chat icon — quick-jump to team chat.
  • Notification Bell — view unread notifications with a badge count.
  • Theme Toggle — switch between light and dark mode.
  • Avatar / Sign Out — click your avatar to go to Settings; click the logout icon to sign out.

1.3 Roles & Permissions

Each user has a role within an organization. Roles control what actions you can perform across all modules.

RoleDescriptionKey Permissions
Owner Full control over the organization and all its data Create/delete anything, manage members, change organization settings, view all audit logs
Admin Manages most content and members but cannot delete the organization Invite/remove members, manage projects and finance settings, approve tasks
Member Standard contributor — can create and edit content Create projects, tasks, transactions, and logs; comment; upload files
Viewer Read-only access to organization data View projects, tasks, reports, and field data; cannot create or modify records
Note Your role is shown on the Settings page. Contact your organization Owner or Admin if you need elevated access.

1.4 Organizations

All data in MetricBase belongs to an Organization. An organization is your company's or team's shared workspace. You may be a member of more than one organization — use the organization selector in the top bar to switch.

  • Each organization has its own projects, financial accounts, field nodes, and team members.
  • Your role may differ between organizations (e.g. Owner in one, Member in another).
  • Settings such as base currency and timezone are configured per organization.

Joining an organization

Ask an Owner or Admin to send you an invite link. Click the link in your email and sign in (or create an account) to accept. Once accepted you will immediately see the organization's data in the app.

Chapter 2

Workspace Home

The Workspace Home is your organization's command center. It aggregates live data from every module into a single view. Navigate to it by clicking the MetricBase logo in the top bar or visiting /home.

Greeting & Date

Time-aware greeting (morning/afternoon/evening) with today's date and a count of items that need your attention.

Today at a Glance — KPIs

Six live metric tiles (projects on track, finance accounts, field nodes, flows, my tasks, transactions) each with a sparkline trend and delta indicator.

Your Apps

Launch cards for every module — shows status, three key metrics, and a colored top border matching the app. Star cards to pin them. Click to open the app.

Recent Activity

Chronological feed of changes made across all modules by all team members, with relative timestamps and deep links.

Tasks & Alerts

Your personally assigned open tasks with due date tone indicators (overdue = red, today = amber, future = neutral).

Quick Access — Pinned

One-click shortcuts to key sections of each module (Finance overview, Projects overview, Field overview, Tools).

Chapter 3

ProBase — Projects

ProBase is the project management module. Use it to plan work, assign tasks, track progress through multiple views, and coordinate team deliverables.

3.1 Projects

A Project groups related tasks under a single goal. Each project belongs to a workspace (organization).

Creating a project

  1. Navigate to ProBase → Projects in the App Switcher.
  2. Click New Project (top-right button).
  3. Enter a project name, optional description, and choose a color.
  4. Click Create. The project opens immediately.

Project header

At the top of every project page you will see:

  • Progress bar — percentage of parent tasks with Done status.
  • Member avatars — workspace members who have access.
  • Save as Template (template icon) — convert this project into a reusable template.
  • New Task button — quickly add a task to this project.

3.2 Tasks & Subtasks

Tasks are the core unit of work. Every task belongs to exactly one project.

Task fields

FieldDescription
TitleShort, descriptive name for the task
StatusTODO · IN_PROGRESS · IN_REVIEW · DONE · CANCELLED
PriorityLOW · MEDIUM · HIGH · URGENT
AssigneeThe team member responsible for completing the task
Due DateTarget completion date. Overdue tasks are highlighted in red.
Start DateWhen work begins (used in Timeline view)
LabelsOne or more project-scoped color-coded tags
DescriptionMarkdown-formatted detail, instructions, or notes
SubtasksChild tasks nested under this task; appear indented in List and Timeline views
Blocked ByLink to one or more tasks that must be done first
RecurrenceRepeat pattern (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly) with optional end date
CommentsThreaded discussion with markdown support
AttachmentsFiles linked via OneDrive integration

Task detail panel

Click any task card or row to open the detail panel on the right side. All fields are editable inline. Changes save automatically. Use Delete Task (at the bottom of the panel) to permanently remove a task — this action cannot be undone.

Tip — subtasks In the detail panel, click Add subtask to break a task into smaller pieces. Subtasks are visible in List and Timeline views when the parent row is expanded. The parent task shows a subtask count badge.

3.3 Views

Switch between views using the tab row beneath the project header. Each view shows the same tasks in a different layout.

Board (Kanban)

Drag-and-drop cards across status columns (TODO → IN_PROGRESS → IN_REVIEW → DONE). Click a column's + button to add a task with that status. The default view for new projects.

List

Flat table with one row per task. Subtasks expand below the parent. Supports inline status, priority, assignee, and due date editing. Useful for bulk review.

Calendar

Monthly calendar showing tasks on their due dates. Click a task to open its detail panel. Tasks without due dates do not appear here.

Timeline (Gantt)

Horizontal bar chart from start date to due date. Drag bars to shift dates. Expand rows to see subtasks inline. Ideal for scheduling and spotting conflicts.

S-Curve

Cumulative task completion graph over time. A classic project management view for comparing planned vs. actual progress. Useful for reporting to stakeholders.

Finance Card

At the top of every project page, a finance summary card shows the linked financial account balance and recent transactions if a finance account is associated with this project.

3.4 Milestones & Labels

Milestones mark key dates or deliverables in a project. Create a milestone from the project settings and assign a due date. Milestones appear in Timeline view.

Labels are project-scoped colored tags. Create labels from the project settings and apply one or more to any task for filtering and visual grouping.

3.5 Templates

Templates save a project structure (tasks, statuses, priorities) as a reusable blueprint for future projects.

  • Save as template — click the template icon in the project header. Give it a name. The template is stored at organization level.
  • Create from template — when creating a new project, select a template from the library. All tasks are copied with the same structure; assignees and dates are cleared.

3.6 AI Features

ProBase includes AI-powered assistance powered by the configured AI agent for your organization.

  • Task Breakdown — on any task, click the AI button to automatically decompose it into a set of subtasks based on the task title and description.
  • Comment Summary — on tasks with many comments, use the AI summary button to get a concise summary of the discussion.
  • Agent Actions — the platform's AI agent can read and create tasks based on natural language instructions given in the Chat module.
Note AI features require an AI agent to be configured for your organization. Ask your Owner to set this up in Settings → Agents.

My Tasks page

Navigate to ProBase → My Tasks to see all tasks assigned to you across every project in the organization, sorted by due date. If you are an Owner or Admin, you also see tasks in IN_REVIEW status that are awaiting approval.

ProBase Dashboard

The ProBase home dashboard (ProBase → Dashboard) shows four summary stats (Total Tasks, In Progress, Overdue, Projects), your task list, a project list, and an overdue tasks alert section.

Chapter 4

Finance — MetricBase

The Finance module provides multi-currency bookkeeping, budgeting, investment tracking, and financial reports for your organization.

4.1 Accounts

An Account represents a financial account such as a bank account, cash wallet, crypto wallet, brokerage account, or credit card.

Account types

TypeTypical use
BankChecking or savings accounts at a bank
CashPhysical cash holdings
CryptoCryptocurrency wallets or exchange accounts
BrokerageStock or securities trading accounts with investment position tracking
CreditCredit cards or lines of credit
ProjectBudget pool linked to a ProBase project
OtherAny other financial holding

Creating an account

  1. Go to Finance → Accounts.
  2. Click New Account.
  3. Choose the account type, enter its name and currency, and set an opening balance.
  4. Click Create.
Note Setting an accurate opening balance is important. The opening balance is the starting point for all balance calculations in reports.

4.2 Transactions

A Transaction is any money movement recorded in an account. Three kinds exist:

  • Income — money received into an account (e.g. salary, client payment).
  • Expense — money paid out of an account (e.g. supplier invoice, subscription).
  • Transfer — movement of money between two accounts you own (e.g. bank to cash).

Logging a transaction

  1. Open an account or go to Finance → Transactions.
  2. Click New Transaction or the + button.
  3. Select the transaction type (Income / Expense / Transfer).
  4. Enter the amount, currency, date, and category.
  5. Add an optional memo for your own reference.
  6. Click Save. The account balance updates immediately.

Multi-currency

If a transaction is in a currency different from the account's base currency, enter the transaction currency and amount. The system will auto-fill the FX rate from the rate table. You can override the rate manually. The base-currency amount is calculated automatically and used in reports.

Approval workflow

Members can create transactions in Pending status. Owners and Admins review and move them to Posted. Only posted transactions appear in reports and balances by default.

Importing transactions

Go to the account page and click Import CSV. Download the template, fill in your rows (date, description, amount, category), then upload. Preview the import before confirming.

4.3 Categories & Budgets

Categories classify your income and expenses for reporting. They can be nested (parent → child).

  • Create and manage categories in Finance → Settings → Categories.
  • Assign a monthly budget to any expense category. The dashboard shows actual vs. budget with colour-coded alerts when you are over budget.
  • Categories apply per organization and are shared by all members.

4.4 Recurring Transactions

Recurring rules automatically post transactions on a schedule so you don't need to log regular payments (rent, subscriptions, payroll) manually.

Setting up a recurring rule

  1. Go to Finance → Recurring.
  2. Click New Rule.
  3. Choose the frequency: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly, and set the interval (e.g. every 2 weeks).
  4. Set the start date and optionally an end date.
  5. Fill in the transaction details (account, amount, category, memo).
  6. Click Save. The system's scheduled job will post a transaction each time the rule fires.

4.5 Investments

Available on Brokerage and Crypto accounts, investment tracking records positions (holdings) and lots (individual buy/sell trades).

  • Position — a holding in a particular symbol (e.g. BBCA shares, BTC tokens). Tracks total quantity held and average cost basis.
  • Lot — an individual buy trade that contributes to a position. Tracks acquisition date, unit price, fees, and remaining quantity.
  • Sell trade — records units sold, sale price, and fees. Updates the lot's remaining quantity and calculates realized P&L.
  • Dividend — log a dividend payment from a position, credited back to the account as income.
  • Close position — marks all lots as fully exited and archives the position.

4.6 Multi-Currency & FX Rates

Your organization has a base currency (set in Finance → Settings). All reports convert values to this currency. FX rates are fetched automatically from the Frankfurter exchange rate API for the transaction date.

  • View and manually override rates in Finance → Settings → FX Rates.
  • Rates are cached by date so historical reports remain consistent.
  • The base currency can be changed at any time; this affects future calculations but not stored transaction amounts.

4.7 Reports & Dashboards

The Finance dashboard and report pages give you financial insight at a glance.

Dashboard

Top-level summary: total balance, income, expenses, and net cashflow. Trend charts and budget status across all accounts.

P&L Statement

Profit & Loss report grouped by category with month-over-month variance. Choose a period (MTD, QTD, YTD, custom range).

Balance Sheet

Snapshot of assets and liabilities at a chosen date. Accounts are grouped by type.

Category Breakdown

Donut chart showing how spending or income is distributed across categories for the selected period.

Cashflow Bar Chart

Monthly bar chart of income vs. expenses. Shows net positive/negative flow per month.

Sankey Diagram

Flow visualization showing how money moves between accounts and categories. Useful for seeing the big picture of fund flows.

Balance Line Chart

Account balance trend over a selected date range. Compare multiple accounts on one chart.

Activity Log

Full audit trail of all changes in the Finance module. Filterable by action type, entity, member, and date range.

Tip — period picker All report views include a period picker. Presets include This month (MTD), This quarter (QTD), Year to date (YTD), Last 3 months, Last 6 months, and Last 12 months. You can also enter a custom date range.
Chapter 5

FieldFlow — Field Operations

FieldFlow manages crude oil production, inter-node transfers, tanker liftings, and production targets. It is designed for upstream field operations teams.

5.1 Nodes

A Node represents a physical or logical production point — a wellhead cluster, tank, pipeline segment, or facility. Nodes are the backbone of FieldFlow: flows and transfers move between nodes.

FieldDescription
CodeShort unique identifier (e.g. WH-01)
NameDescriptive name of the node
TypeClassification (wellhead, tank, segment, etc.)
CapacityMaximum volume and unit (e.g. 50,000 bbl)
Opening StockStarting inventory when the node was registered
Parent NodeParent in the node hierarchy (for aggregated reporting)
ActiveToggle to include / exclude from daily flow logging
NotesFree-text operational notes

Creating a node

  1. Go to FieldFlow → Nodes.
  2. Click New Node.
  3. Fill in Code, Name, Type, Capacity, and optional parent.
  4. Click Save.

5.2 Flows (Production Records)

A Flow is a dated production entry on a specific node. Log one flow per node per day (or as needed). Flows track inflow, outflow, and stock movement volumes.

FieldDescription
NodeThe production point being logged
DateDate of the flow record
InflowVolume entering the node (e.g. gross production)
OutflowVolume leaving the node (e.g. dispatched)
Water Cut %Percentage of water in the produced fluid
CategoryClassification tag (production, export, injection, etc.)
StatusActual or Forecast
ReporterTeam member who entered this record
Tip — bulk import Use the CSV import on the Flows page to upload multiple daily records at once. Download the template, populate it with your production data, and upload. The system validates dates and node codes before importing.

Transfers

A Transfer records the movement of crude between two nodes — for example from a tank farm to a marine terminal. Log the Nominated Volume (planned) and Receipt Volume (actual). The system calculates the loss or gain automatically.

5.3 Liftings

A Lifting is a tanker cargo operation. Record a lifting when a vessel arrives at your terminal to load crude.

FieldDescription
Vessel NameName of the tanker
BuyerCargo buyer (company or entity)
Source NodeThe terminal or tank farm the crude is lifted from
StatusTentative → Confirmed → Loading → Completed
LaycanLoading window: earliest and latest dates the vessel may arrive
Nominated VolumeVolume originally nominated by the buyer
BL VolumeBill of Lading volume — actual loaded volume
CQD VolumeCalculated quantity from discharge survey
ETAEstimated time of arrival
Start/Stop LoadActual loading start and end times

Lifting lifecycle

Move a lifting through its status stages as operations progress: Tentative → Confirmed → Loading → Completed. Each status change is logged in the audit trail.

5.4 Production Targets

Targets set monthly production goals by node and category. Compare targets against actual flows in the analytics dashboard.

  • Go to FieldFlow → Targets.
  • Select a year and month, choose a node and category, enter the target volume.
  • The system enforces uniqueness: one target per node + category + year-month.

5.5 Custom Dashboards & Formulas

Advanced users can build personal analytics dashboards and define reusable custom formulas.

Custom dashboards

In FieldFlow → Dashboards, drag and drop widgets onto a canvas to build a dashboard tailored to your reporting needs. Widgets include production charts, lifting summaries, target vs. actual comparisons, and formula outputs. Save multiple dashboards and set one as your default.

User formulas

The formula library lets you define named calculations using a bracket-reference DSL. Formulas can reference node flow series and perform mathematical operations. Saved formulas are reusable across dashboards.

Field-to-Projects integration

Field operations can automatically create tasks in ProBase. When a new lifting is logged, for example, the system can create a corresponding task in your project plan. Tasks created this way carry a soft link back to the field entity (node, flow, lifting, or target) so you can navigate between the operational and planning views.

#CalculatorWhat it computes

3API Gravity ConverterConverts °API to/from specific gravity; classifies crude (light/medium/heavy/extra-heavy)

Tip Results update in real time as you type. The formula used for each calculation is shown beneath the result so you can verify the method. Preset values are available for common scenarios.
Chapter 7

Chat

The Chat module provides real-time team messaging organized into channels. All messages are scoped to your organization.

Channels

  • Public channels — visible and joinable by all organization members.
  • Private channels — invitation-only; only members can see the channel and its messages.
  • Navigate between channels using the sidebar in the Chat view.
  • Channels show an unread message indicator when there are new messages since your last visit.

Creating a channel

  1. In the Chat sidebar, click + New Channel.
  2. Enter a channel name (lowercase, no spaces — use hyphens).
  3. Choose Public or Private.
  4. Click Create.

AI Agent in Chat

If an AI Agent is configured for your organization, you can interact with it directly in Chat. The agent can read your organization's tasks, answer questions about projects and field data, and create or update records based on your instructions.

Note — permissions Your role determines whether you can send messages and create channels. Viewers can read messages but cannot post. Members can post. Admins and Owners can create and manage channels.
Chapter 8

Settings & Profile

Access Settings by clicking your avatar in the top-right corner, or navigating to /settings.

User Profile

  • Name — your display name shown in greetings, task assignments, and activity feeds.
  • Email — your sign-in email address.
  • Title / Bio — optional professional title and short biography visible to team members.
  • Profile Image — avatar shown in the top bar, task assignments, and comments. Linked from Google or Microsoft OAuth automatically.

Telegram Integration

Connect your Telegram account to receive reminders and alerts via the MetricBase bot.

  1. Go to Settings → Telegram.
  2. A unique verification code is displayed.
  3. Open Telegram, find the MetricBase bot, and send /start <your-code>.
  4. The account is linked automatically. Future reminders arrive in Telegram.

Organization settings (Owners & Admins)

  • Members — invite new members by email, change member roles, or remove members.
  • Base currency — the reporting currency for Finance (default: IDR).
  • Timezone — used for scheduled jobs and report period boundaries.
  • Color — organization accent color shown in the app switcher.
  • AI Agents — configure one or more AI agents with custom instructions, choose the AI model, and enable or disable agents for the organization.
Warning Removing a member immediately revokes their access to all organization data. This action cannot be undone from the UI — the user would need to be re-invited.
Chapter 9

Notifications & Activity

MetricBase keeps you informed about changes that affect you or your team.

Notification bell

The bell icon in the top bar shows a badge count of unread notifications. Click it to open the notification panel. Each notification shows:

  • The module it came from (ProBase, Finance, FieldFlow, etc.)
  • What happened and who triggered it
  • A relative timestamp (e.g. "3 minutes ago")
  • A direct link to the relevant record

Click a notification to mark it as read and navigate to the source. Click Mark all read to clear the badge.

Activity feeds

Two levels of activity feed exist:

  • Workspace Home — shows all recent changes across all modules by all team members, in reverse chronological order.
  • Finance Activity Log (Settings → Activity) — a filterable, paginated audit trail of every Finance action: transactions created, categories changed, members invited, etc. Filterable by action type, entity, member, and date range. Available to Owners and Admins only.

Email digest

The platform sends a scheduled email digest summarizing recent activity. The digest is sent by the cron scheduler. Contact your administrator to adjust the digest frequency.
